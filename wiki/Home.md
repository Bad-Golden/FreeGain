# FreeGain Wiki

**FreeGain** is free feedback suppression for live vocal mics. It listens
to your vocal mic and to the signal going to your speakers, learns how the
room carries the speakers back into the mic, and subtracts that in real
time. That gives you more gain before feedback and less speaker spill in
the vocal.

It runs on **Windows 10/11** and **macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer**. It works
with **any mixing console** whose audio reaches the computer, and can
remote-control 36 console models from Behringer, Midas, Yamaha, Allen &
Heath and Soundcraft, plus any OSC console.

> **Beta software.** FreeGain has an automated test suite but hasn't been
> proven on a live rig yet. Try it at soundcheck before relying on it in a
> show.
